Ambika Motors - single page website

Open index.html in a browser to preview. Upload the whole folder to any static host to go live.

Before publishing:
- Replace https://www.example.com in index.html (canonical), sitemap.xml and robots.txt with the real domain.
- Replace the illustrative car photos in /assets with real photos of the lot and stock when available.
- Confirm the document list and opening hours with the owner.
- Review privacy-policy.html and terms.html with the owner (not legal advice).

Colour palette
- A "Colours" button (bottom-left) opens the palette panel. Choose a ready-made palette or pick your own
  Main / Dark / Background colours, check the preview, then press "Apply to website".
- It recolours every page and is remembered in that browser.
- To make a choice permanent for all visitors: press "Copy CSS" and paste the values into :root in styles.css.
- To hide the button from visitors: set SHOW_PICKER = false at the top of theme.js (open any page with ?palette=1 to see it again).

Animation and video notes
- Sections fade, slide or wipe in as you scroll; headings reveal word by word with a soft blur; a thin scroll-progress bar sits under the header; a moving ticker strip follows the hero; car cards get a rotating accent border and primary buttons a light sweep on hover; FAQ items and checklist ticks animate open. All of it is turned off automatically for visitors who prefer reduced motion, and the site works normally with JavaScript off.
- Three short clips are included: the hero background, a full-width lot band and a small clip in the "Selling your car" column. All are illustrative and not footage of current stock.
- Clips are muted, looped and only load when scrolled into view. Replace assets/video/*.mp4 with real footage of the lot when available and update the poster images in /assets.
