What to check on your visit

A good used car purchase starts with a careful look. Use this list when you come in, whichever car you choose.

  • Body and paintLook for uneven panel gaps, repainted areas and rust around the doors and boot.
  • TyresCheck tread depth and whether wear is even on both sides.
  • Engine startStart the car cold if you can and listen for unusual sounds.
  • Interior and ACTest the air conditioning, windows, lights and seat adjustments.
  • Test driveFeel the clutch, brakes and steering in real traffic, not just in the lane.
  • Service historyAsk for service records and compare the odometer with them.
05

Documents to keep ready

Having these in hand keeps the transfer simple. Confirm the exact list with us on the phone before you visit.

If you are selling

  • Original Registration Certificate (RC)
  • Valid insurance policy
  • Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate
  • Owner ID and address proof
  • Loan closure letter / NOC, if the car was financed
  • Service records and spare key, if available

If you are buying

  • ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN or passport)
  • Address proof for RC transfer
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Your valid driving licence for the test drive
